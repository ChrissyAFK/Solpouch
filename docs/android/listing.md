## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
Solpouch gives every purchase a limit.

A pouch is a small wallet with rules. It holds what you put in, spends only at the stores you allow, and shows what is left today. Make one for groceries, one for takeout, one for a project, and keep each budget apart from the others.

HOW IT WORKS
• Pick a pouch and say or type what you need. Solpouch searches the store, builds a cart, and asks when something is unclear.
• Every line is checked against the pouch rules before anything is paid.
• You approve the exact order, then the pouch pays. If you choose, a pouch can pay small exact matches on its own.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Per-order and daily limits for every pouch.
• A list of allowed stores for every pouch.
• Freeze a pouch with one tap. Nothing can be paid from it until you unfreeze it.
• Refills are owner-only and wait out a waiting period first, so a budget cannot be topped up on impulse.
• The assistant can ask questions and build a cart. It cannot pay on its own and it cannot refill a pouch.

BUILT ON SOLANA
Each pouch is its own on-chain vault holding USDC, so the limits are enforced by the vault itself, not only by the app.

PLEASE NOTE
Solpouch currently runs on the Solana devnet with test USDC. No real money is used. It is a working preview of the product.

Sign-in uses your Google account. You can delete your account and its data at any time from Profile. Details: https://solpouch.tech/delete-account
```
